## Data and safety boundaries

`saveWeeklyGoalDays` validates the offered 2/3/4 values and uses the existing profile store. Progress adds read-only `currentLocalDay` and `participationLocalDays` values derived from existing qualifying records. The UI renders actual dates, area preferences, participation, plan timing, and available levels. It does not invent completion days or check-in answers. Why-plan reasons are attributed to their area; global reasons appear once.

Conditional safety continues to use `requiresConditionalSafetyAnswer` after the domain-required change and movement answers are collected. The canvas's illustrative links do not replace service preconditions. Selection, Attention resolution, persistence, account sync, and routine-clock implementations remain underneath the new presentation.
