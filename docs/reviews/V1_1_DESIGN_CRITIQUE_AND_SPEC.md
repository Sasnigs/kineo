# Kineo v1 → v1.1 design critique and specification

**Status:** Design-only critique and proposed v1.1 specification; no implementation authorized in this pass.  
**Date:** 2026-10-15  
**Decision:** Iterate before production UI handoff. The v1 direction is substantially more ownable than the baseline, but its repeated card grammar and weak safety/action hierarchy are not at a 9.2/10 craft bar.

## Evidence and measurement boundary

This review compares the requested numbered screens in both `/Users/chiemerieonyeanusi/Pictures/Kineo-frontend/after/v1/light` and `/Users/chiemerieonyeanusi/Pictures/Kineo-frontend/after/v1/dark`—01, 11, 16, 19, 25, 31, 36, 37, 47, 52, and 69—with representative original screens in `/Users/chiemerieonyeanusi/Pictures/Kineo-frontend/b4` (welcome, area selection, plan, and routine). **Important distinction:** the v1 images are exports of a 390 CSS-pixel-wide HTML design canvas, not verified 402 × 874-point iPhone simulator captures of the current React Native app. Its CSS uses 23px side padding, 19px panel padding, 16px choice padding, 20px panel radius, and 15px choice radius. The React Native code is an earlier implementation with different values: `screenHorizontal: 20`, `radius.card: 24`, `radius.option: 18`, and `controlMinimumHeight: 52`. Numbers below identify which system they describe; proposed 402 × 874 measurements are target layout calculations, not claims about an unmeasured simulator render. Static screenshots cannot establish press feedback, animation, screen-reader output, or selected/error states.

The baseline has an earnest, accessible product voice and clear safety escape, but its very large repeated bordered blocks and placeholder movement media flatten the experience. V1 improves editorial confidence, tonal warmth, and light/dark consistency. Preserve its restrained forest/cream/lime palette, non-competitive progress language, and clear Quick-versus-Standard explanation. Do not preserve every container treatment simply because those improvements live inside them.

## 1. AI-aesthetic and “boring UI” tell audit

1. **Every screen opens with the same tracked-out uppercase eyebrow.** Welcome (01), Today (16), plan (19), question (25), withheld (31), feedback (47), History (52), and Data Controls (69) all use a small all-caps pre-title, often followed by a very large headline. This makes radically different moments—orientation, decision, warning, and record-keeping—sound and look identical. In the RN implementation `PageHeader` requires an `eyebrow`, the shared `eyebrow` token is 12pt with 1.5pt tracking, and the account entry repeats the pattern. **V1.1:** reserve an eyebrow for genuine state/progress (“Step 2 of 4”), not a default component slot. Give Today a date/context line, questions a concise area label, safety a plain-language status, and settings a direct title. Use sentence case unless the short code-like label is essential.
2. **“SaaS card soup” replaces information architecture.** Focus choices (11), plan metadata/reason/steps (19), withheld notice/action (31), History (52), and privacy actions (69) repeatedly alternate white or near-black 1px-bordered rounded containers. The RN styles compound this through 24pt `todayCard`, `planReasonCard`, `historyCard`, `safetyCard`, and `consistencyMeter`. Equal radii, borders, and padding erase the distinction between a decision, a record, an explanatory aside, and a destructive action. **V1.1:** use one true focal surface per screen at most. Let routine metadata and history rows sit on the canvas with dividers; reserve raised/filled surfaces for a major decision or a durable warning. Do not solve this by merely changing shadow strength.
3. **The badge–label–chevron formula is overextended.** V1 choice cards repeatedly use circular direction icons and a second selection circle or arrow (11, 25, 47); RN `ChoiceButton` adds a 40pt tinted icon badge and right chevron, while `ListRow` applies the same badge/chevron language to navigation. These signals conflict: chevron says “opens a new page,” radio says “selects this answer.” The up/down icons on better/worse answers can also imply an evaluative score. **V1.1:** use actual radio/check affordances only for single-choice answers, a chevron only for navigation, and pictograms only when they add meaning. No decorative badge on every row. Preserve one tap target across the full row.
4. **Primary decisions and secondary metadata share a plane.** The plan (19) spreads intensity level, duration, step count, “why,” a duration switch, and movement preview across similar visual weights; the key rationale appears below the selector instead of immediately after the proposed plan. Timer complete (36) and between-movement (37) place “Something feels wrong” as the quietest bottom action. Withheld (31) makes “View my history” visually easier to find than support guidance. Data Controls (69) gives export, reset, and deletion nearly the same row treatment. **V1.1:** sequence the plan as recommendation → one-sentence reason → duration decision → preview; make the safety exit persistently legible without turning it into the primary CTA; separate destructive actions physically and semantically from routine settings.
5. **The generic wellness “glow/orb” can become an AI-product cliché.** Welcome (01) has a pleasant abstract movement glow but little evidence of the actual Kineo product. **V1.1:** pair expressive illustration with a truthful preview of the experience: an annotated Today decision, a movement instruction/timer state, and an honest history snippet. This should show what Kineo does without inventing clinical measurement. Keep any illustrative movement media labeled as prototype until reviewed media and instruction copy are ready.
6. **Dark mode is a recolor rather than a fully rebalanced hierarchy.** The near-black canvas and dark-green cards are attractive, but border-defined option/radio states in 11, 25, and 47 become visually subtle; lime appears on primary actions, links, and “Something feels wrong,” weakening semantic distinction. **V1.1:** design dark elevation and state contrast intentionally: distinct canvas/surface/elevated roles, brighter text for decision labels, a visible 2pt selected outline/fill, and a safety treatment that is unmistakable without relying on hue alone. Measure contrast in rendered states before sign-off; this review does **not** assert a WCAG failure from screenshots alone.

## 2. Padding, margin, and rhythm audit for 402 × 874pt

The approved UX specification calls for a 402 × 874pt reference viewport, 20pt screen margins, an 8/12/16/24/32pt gap scale, 16pt control radius, 24pt card radius, 44pt minimum targets, and 52pt minimum controls. V1’s HTML canvas is 390px wide, with `.content { padding: 18px 23px 24px }`, `.panel { padding: 19px; margin: 18px; border-radius: 20px }`, `.choice { padding: 18px 16px; margin: 10px; min-height: 72px }`, and `.tabs { padding: 12px 12px 22px }`. Those values are internally close but not on the approved spacing scale. At the target viewport, `402 − 2×20 = 362pt` usable width.

| Location / measured source | Current rhythm problem | Exact v1.1 rule and reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Canvas shell: 23px sides on 390px; RN shell: 20pt sides | Two design systems imply different line length. V1 exports give 344px content width; the target RN shell gives 362pt. A straight visual transplant will change wraps and card heights. | Use 20pt target margins consistently. Test title wraps and CTA visibility at 402 × 874, 375 × 812, and 320pt width, rather than tuning to the HTML export. Keep the native safe area outside this calculation. |
| RN 24pt card padding inside 20pt margins | A full-width card leaves `362 − 48 = 314pt` interior text width. Another icon/chevron row can leave only about 224–240pt for labels, creating needless two-line wraps and very tall rows. | Use 20pt padding for the one focal card; 16pt for compact grouped rows; 24pt only where copy needs a distinct, quiet reading surface. Let decision copy take the width before allocating icon furniture. |
| RN `ChoiceButton`: 16pt horizontal padding, 40pt icon, two 16pt gaps, ≈18pt chevron | At 402pt, the label’s approximate measure is `362 − 32 − 40 − 32 − 18 = 240pt`. On focus/feedback screens this is space spent on ornament and duplicate state symbols. | Single-select row: 16pt inset, text first, one 22–24pt radio/check on the trailing edge, 12pt label-to-control gap, min 56pt high (grow with Dynamic Type). Full-row press target. For navigational rows, use a chevron but no radio. |
| Canvas `.choice` 18×16px padding, 72px minimum and 10px external margin | Three near-identical answer slabs consume at least 236px before section gaps, while Focus (11) and Feedback (47) leave conspicuous unstructured space below. | Use a grouped choice list with 1pt dividers or a 12pt inter-row gap only when options need independent visual cards. Do not add a large blank spacer simply to fill the screen; place contextual reassurance near the choice group and let the footer remain calm. |
| Canvas `.panel` margin 18px and `.hero` margin 21px vs RN 20/24/32 gaps | Adjacent sections drift between 18, 20, 21, 24, and 32 without signaling hierarchy. | Canvas header `topline` 24px bottom gap; eyebrow `4px 0 12px`; RN `header` gap 8pt | Stacking progress, eyebrow, 30–40pt title, and explanatory copy creates header-heavy question screens (25, 47) while the answer list begins too low. | Header template by job: progress + area (one compact line), 8–12pt to question, 12–16pt to helpful body if needed, then 24pt to choices. | Routine media: RN `aspectRatio: 1.16`; canvas media visually dominates 36/37 | At 362pt wide, a 1.16 aspect ratio produces ≈312pt media height before headings, timer, safety cue, controls, and safe areas. A large placeholder can crowd the bottom action stack and push safety affordance away. | Use an adaptive media height (target ≈220–260pt at 402 × 874, depending on instruction length) with a text-first instruction fallback. Timer and next action must remain above the safe-area footer; safety action stays directly below primary controls or in a persistent footer slot. Never crop essential movement guidance. |
| Fixed bottom bar / tabs: RN min 62pt plus safe-area; HTML tabs pad 12/12/22px | The fixed bar compresses the scrollable viewport. On 11/25/47 the concept instead leaves ~one to two large card-heights of dead zone below choices, while dense 19/36 can risk footer overlap. Use scroll content bottom padding of `bar height + safe-area + 16pt` when overlaying; otherwise keep it in normal layout. Use one footer action on decision screens, not a floating pile. Validate keyboard and 200% text scaling. |
| Icon containers: RN 40pt list badges and 52pt Today/plan badges, often nested in 24pt cards | Badges create large left indent and apparent alignment changes between cards, rows, and copy. These are target constraints, not a request to change `tokens.ts` wholesale. Reuse existing semantic values where they already meet the rule; introduce or rename tokens only after a v1.1 screen layout proves the need. Preserve Dynamic Type, localization room, system appearance, safe areas, and the fixed-bottom-bar accessible layout behavior already present in RN.

### Content and trust rules carried into v1.1

- Say what a recommendation is based on in everyday language; never say a level diagnoses, measures recovery, or was caused by prior routine completion. The app is general wellness planning, not medical care.
- On “routine withheld,” name the user-described change and the product boundary. Provide appropriate professional-support guidance; do not route the user back into another routine via a visually dominant action. The safety gate must remain persistent according to the UX contract.
- “Something feels wrong” must be available throughout the routine and paused state; confirmation of an accidental tap must never silently bypass an intentional stop.
- Timer completion means the timer ended, not that the user completed the movement. Preserve explicit Continue/Skip/Alternative/Pause choices and record them accurately.
- Feedback remains optional and phrased as the user’s own report; History groups events without causal inference. Participation days count the approved kinds of participation equally, without streak pressure.
- Privacy actions state scope before confirmation. Reset History retains the selected areas, reminders, and any current Attention Required gate; full deletion is separate and appropriately destructive. The export explanation should state where the file goes and how long the one-time server copy lasts.
- Follow system appearance by default and support explicit Light/Dark/System selection in the eventual product. Verify both palettes and transitions; the screenshots show light and dark but not a functioning theme selector.

## 4. Anchored 10-point rubric and v1.1 acceptance target

**Scoring method:** each dimension has five observable criteria worth 0–2 points each: 0 = absent/contradictory, 1 = present but inconsistent, 2 = coherent and verified in all relevant sampled states. Half-points represent partial evidence. The v1 scores are evidence-based design judgments, not user-test results; motion is provisional because screenshots are static. **Gate for v1.1:** each of the six dimensions must score **at least 9.2/10**, not merely a 9.2 average. A single unresolved High-severity safety or destructive-action issue blocks sign-off regardless of mean.

| Dimension (five criteria, each 0–2) | V1 score /10 | Evidence for current score | What earns ≥9.2 in v1.1 |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Visual Identity & Anti-Slop — ownable voice, compositional variety, meaningful iconography, product truth, restraint | **6.0** | Forest/lime palette and friendly copy are distinctive; repeated eyebrow, card, badge, and chevron formulas are not. Welcome art is more atmosphere than product preview. | At least four visibly different screen compositions; every badge/icon has a job; no default eyebrow/card wrapper; welcome previews a truthful Kineo decision and routine state; no borrowed product-specific composition. |
| Spatial Rhythm & Padding — grid, measure, section spacing, viewport fit, safe-area behavior | **6.5** | Canvas 390px and RN 402pt target diverge; 18/19/21px gaps drift from token scale; 24pt nested card padding narrows text; static frames show both dead zones and dense footer risk. | 20pt outer grid at 402pt; documented 8/12/16/24/32 rhythm; no choice label measure under ~260pt without a demonstrated need; first-screen decision and CTA fit at 874pt; no footer overlap at 320–402pt and large text. |
| Typography & Hierarchy — headline purpose, decision prominence, copy measure, metadata rank, Dynamic Type | **6.5** | Large headings read well, but universal tracked caps and equal-strength metadata make question, warning, and plan sound alike. Dynamic Type has not been visually proven in v1 canvas. | Distinct header variants; plan reason adjacent to recommendation; primary action and safety action scan immediately; sentence-case UI except true labels; long labels, 200% text, and localization retain reading order. |
| Surface Depth & Light/Dark Craft — elevation logic, borders, semantic color, state contrast, system theme | **7.0** | Both palettes feel coherent; many identical bordered dark/white panels flatten depth, and dark radio states look subtle. System switching is not shown. | ≤1 focal elevated plane per ordinary screen; dividers/plain canvas for secondary content; measured text/control contrast in all states; dark selected/focus states identifiable without hue; Light/Dark/System verified end to end. |
| Motion & Tactile Feel — purpose, feedback, continuity, interruptibility, reduced motion | **4.0 provisional** | Static canvas cannot verify motion. RN shows opacity press feedback and reduced-motion handling for routine video, but no v1.1 transition vocabulary or recorded gesture/timer behavior has been reviewed. | Supply interaction specs and actual capture for choice selection, tab change, plan duration, timer completion, pause/resume, and safety interruption. | Ergonomics & Accessibility — target size, one-handed reach, semantic control, focus/VO, safety prominence | **6.0** | RN has 52pt controls and an accessible tab layout, but concept 42px icon button is below the 44pt spec; choice semantics conflict; safety link is quiet on routine screens. Selected/focus states are unverified. | All targets ≥44pt and key actions ≥52pt; semantic radio/navigation roles; visible focus and selected states; VoiceOver labels and order; safety exit visible at every routine state; destructive action separated; 320pt width, keyboard, and 200% text tested. |

**Current overall (simple mean): 6.0/10** (36.0 ÷ 6); not a release score. The ≥9.2 gate is deliberately per-dimension so a beautiful screen cannot compensate for weak safety or inaccessible controls.

### Verification required before scoring v1.1

1. Produce annotated Light, Dark, and System design states for the representative screens plus selection, error, paused, and safety-interruption states. Keep a 402 × 874pt master and test at 375 × 812pt and 320pt width.
2. Measure rendered contrast for body/secondary text, borders that communicate selection, focus indicators, button labels, warnings, and disabled states in both modes. Correct failures; do not infer compliance from a hex palette alone.
3. Record actual interactions or a clickable prototype to judge timing, press response, transition continuity, cancellation/interruption, and reduced-motion behavior. A static image cannot satisfy the motion criterion.
4. Test Dynamic Type to at least 200%, VoiceOver reading/selection, keyboard and safe-area behavior, longer localized labels, and the complete routine action stack with the safety exit visible.
5. Reconcile the canvas IA with the approved Today / Progress / Profile navigation and the existing safety/account state contract before connecting designs to production RN. Review every change against `docs/KINEO_UX_DESIGN_SPEC.md` and `AGENTS.md`.

**Recommended next design deliverable:** a small set of v1.1 master components and the eleven audited screens in both palettes, with interaction/state annotations and measured 402pt layouts. Do not begin implementation from the current v1 images alone.
